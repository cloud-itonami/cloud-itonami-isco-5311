# Business Model: Independent Child Care Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-5311`
- ISCO-08: `5311`
- Occupation: Child Care Workers
- Social impact: child-safety, caregiver-relief, local-jobs

## Customer

- families
- small daycare co-ops
- employers offering childcare benefits

## Offer

- consented intake and scheduling
- supervised activity support
- attendance and incident logging
- guardian reporting

## Revenue

- per-session fee
- monthly practice platform fee
- reporting package

## Trust Controls

- robot never left unsupervised with a child
- any incident triggers immediate human escalation, cannot be suppressed
- care and safety logs are auditable, not editable
